# Medau Marketing Copy Audit & Revisions

## Audit Principles
- Audience: Hospital administrators, clinical directors, behavioral health program managers
- They're skeptical, busy, outcomes-focused
- Kill anything generic enough to appear on any SaaS site
- Be specific to behavioral health — not "healthcare" broadly
- Warm authority, not salesy

---

## Hero

### Current
- Badge: "Built by Physicians • HIPAA Compliant"
- H1: "Smarter behavioral health care, powered by data"
- Sub: "Turn daily check-ins into actionable clinical insights. Real-time monitoring, mood analytics, and patient engagement — built for facilities that refuse to settle."

### Issues
- H1 is generic — "powered by data" could be any healthtech company
- "facilities that refuse to settle" is filler — doesn't say what they're settling for
- "actionable clinical insights" is a cliché in healthcare SaaS

### Revised
- Badge: "Built by Physicians • HIPAA Compliant" *(keep — it's specific and credible)*
- H1: **"Behavioral health analytics that clinicians actually use"**
  - *Why: Addresses the #1 buyer objection — "will my staff adopt it?" — and differentiates from shelfware*
- Sub: **"Real-time safety monitoring, mood tracking, and patient engagement tools — designed alongside psychiatrists for inpatient behavioral health units."**
  - *Why: Names specific use cases, names the specialty (psychiatry/inpatient), removes vague "actionable insights"*
- CTAs: "Request a Demo" / "See Products" *(keep — clear and standard)*

## Summary of Key Changes
1. **Killed generic SaaS language** — "powered by data," "actionable insights," "refuse to settle," "complete toolkit"
2. **Added behavioral health specificity** — "inpatient units," "observation levels," "treatment teams," "bedside," "charge nurses"
3. **Broke the repetitive sentence pattern** — "Built by X. Y by Z." was used 3 times
4. **Made benefits concrete** — instead of "real-time analytics" → "no waiting for end-of-shift reports"
5. **Addressed buyer psychology** — adoption concern (H1), audit trail (compliance), "without the spreadsheets" (pain point)
